1. Introduction

DualMind Arena ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI model comparison platform.

By using DualMind Arena, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used for account authentication and communication
  • Username: Your chosen display name
  • Password: Securely hashed and stored via Supabase authentication

2.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about your interactions with the Service:

  • Prompts and Queries: Text you submit to AI models
  • Votes and Preferences: Your model comparisons and voting history
  • Chat History: Conversations and threads you create
  • Model Selections: Which AI models you choose to compare

2.3 Technical Data

We collect technical information to improve service quality:

  • IP Address: For security and analytics
  • Browser Type and Version: To ensure compatibility
  • Device Information: Operating system, screen size
  • Cookies and Local Storage: For authentication and preferences
  • Usage Analytics: Page views, feature usage, session duration

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide and maintain the AI comparison platform
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and secure your account
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences and chat history
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve features
  • Leaderboards: To calculate and display model rankings
  • Communication: To send service updates and notifications
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third-Party Services

We share data with trusted third-party providers:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database services (see Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • AI Model Providers: Your prompts are sent to selected AI models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.)
  • Analytics Services: Aggregated usage data for service improvement

4.2 Public Information

The following information may be publicly visible:

  • Aggregated voting statistics (no personal identification)
  • Model performance leaderboards
  • Community-driven rankings

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication: Secure password hashing via Supabase
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access to personal data
  • Regular Audits: Security reviews and vulnerability assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Chat History: Stored indefinitely unless you delete specific threads
  • Voting Data: Retained for leaderboard calculations
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated data retained for service improvement

You can delete your account and associated data at any time through your account settings.

Our Mission

To bring the best AI models to everyone, and to improve them through real-world community evaluations.

Our Vision

An open space to try all the best AIs and shape their future through collective, unbiased feedback.

Fair & Transparent

Battles use anonymous model assignment so your votes reflect true quality — not brand familiarity.

Community-Driven

Every vote shapes the leaderboard. Real-world human preferences — not benchmarks — determine rankings.

What DualMind Arena Offers

Compare AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, xAI, Cohere, and more — all in one place:

  • Battle Mode: Anonymous head-to-head AI battles with community voting
  • Arena Mode: Direct model comparisons where you pick both sides
  • Direct Chat: Single-model conversations with full history saved
  • Live Leaderboard: Community-driven rankings updated in real-time
  • Shared Threads: Share your best battles with anyone via a public link
